# Scroll Academy — Gamified Bible Study (Duolingo-style)

A free-first, end-to-end gamified Bible study app — the Scroll Bible alternative with
codex/manuscript study, apologetics, comparative worldviews, and biblical Greek & Hebrew.

## Feature summary

- **7 courses**: Biblical Greek, Biblical Hebrew, Manuscripts & Codices, Apologetics,
  Worldviews & Religions (comparative — Islam, Judaism, Hinduism, Buddhism, atheism, New Age),
  The Big Picture (whole-Bible survey), Study Skills (inductive study methods)
- **Progressive difficulty**: units tier Foundations → Growing → Advanced (timed challenges),
  exercise types escalate from multiple choice to typed answers
- **Full KJV Bible**: all 66 books, highlights (4 colors), bookmarks, notes, verse sharing,
  6 reading plans, +10 XP per chapter read
- **Gamification**: XP & levels, streaks (+ streak freeze), hearts (regen + refill),
  gems economy, daily quests, tiered achievements, weekly leagues, unit chests,
  spaced-repetition mistakes review, daily goal, combo bonuses, double-XP boosts
- **Social**: friends (requests/accept), community feed with likes & comments,
  share lessons/achievements/verses, covenant-grounded community guidelines
- **Avatars**: skin/hair/eyes/outfits/headwear/backdrops + accessories including
  gold, silver & wooden crucifixes, plain cross, and prayer beads
- **UX polish**: haptics (vibration), sound effects, confetti, mascot animations,
  browser notifications, toasts, spring animations, mobile-first responsive layout

## Tech

Next.js 16 (App Router) · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS 4 · shadcn/ui · Prisma + SQLite ·
Framer Motion · TanStack Query · Zustand. Public-domain KJV text.

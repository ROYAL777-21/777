## 1 · The offer (say it exactly like this)

> **"A discipleship plan your whole congregation can actually finish — 5 minutes a day, 18 courses, the original languages included. Flat $99/month for up to 50 seats. You watch it work in the dashboard: who's studying, who's reading, who's gone quiet — and you can cancel any month."**

Why this is a Grand Slam Offer for a pastor (Hormozi's value equation, applied):

| Element | The line that lands |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome | "Your congregation actually reading Scripture daily — not pledging to, doing it" |
| Perceived likelihood | "18 courses, 611 lessons, and the same streak mechanics that keep 500M+ people on language apps" |
| Time delay ↓ | "Live in 10 minutes. The dashboard fills with real data the first week." |
| Effort & sacrifice ↓ | "Your part is one email to the congregation. We handle seats, content, updates." |
| Risk reversal | "Month-to-month. Cancel any month. First month covered by the pilot discount." |

**Pricing (from REVENUE.md):** ≤50 seats **$99/mo** · ≤250 seats **$299/mo** · diocesan/networks (1,000+) **$1,999/mo**. Flat-rate, unlimited hearts and every course for every seat — no per-seat upsells, ever.

## 3 · The five-touch sequence (3 weeks, ~4 hours/week)

### Touch 1 — the research note (email, 10 min/church)
**Subject:** "the [Church Name] reading plan question"

> Pastor [Name] —
>
> I noticed [specific thing: your Genesis series / the midweek group / your post about Biblical literacy]. Quick question: after the sermon ends Sunday, what happens to the reading?
>
> We built Scroll Academy for the gap — 5-minute daily lessons (Greek, the whole-Bible story, apologetics), streaks that keep people going, and a dashboard that shows you who's actually studying. [Church like yours] is running it as their congregation plan.
>
> Can I show you in 15 minutes this week? I'll bring the dashboard on my phone — you'll know in one look whether it fits [Church Name].
>
> — [You] · [phone] · scroll.academy/church

### Touch 2 — the call (48h later, if no reply)
> "Hi Pastor [Name], [You] — I emailed about the reading-plan question. Twenty seconds: we run your congregation's Bible study as an app, five minutes a day, and you see the fruit in a dashboard. Worth fifteen minutes Thursday at 2?"

If no: *"Totally fair. I'll send one email with a 90-second demo video — if it's interesting, it'll find you."* Then stop; touch 3 revives in a week.

### Touch 3 — the 90-second demo video (send to every warm prospect)
Screen-record: the winding path → one lesson (heart hits, sound on) → the church dashboard (cohort progress, verse coverage, the shepherding list). End on: *"Ten pilot churches get the founding rate — $99/month, month-to-month, cancel any month. [Church name] would be #[n]."*

### Touch 4 — the live demo (15 min, the close happens here)
1. **(2 min)** Their name in the dashboard header — "this is your view from day one."
2. **(5 min)** One lesson *together* — they hold the phone. Let the sound effects and hearts do the selling.
3. **(5 min)** The dashboard: verse coverage, streaks, "Worth a call this week" list — *"this is the shepherding list; it writes itself."*
4. **(3 min)** The paper: pricing, seats, the onboarding email they send the congregation. Ask: **"Should we set your 50 seats up for the new year — or start a smaller 25-seat pilot this Advent?"** (Either answer is a close.)

### Touch 5 — the onboarding email (day of signing)
You send it from their account draft — the congregation never waits on the pastor's to-do list:
> Subject: your Scroll Academy seat is ready (5 min/day, every course)
> …link + seat code + "your first lesson takes 4 minutes — the whole Genesis story."

## 4 · Objection handling (the five you'll actually hear)

| Objection | The answer |
|---|---|
| "We already use [RightNow Media / YouVersion]" | "Perfect — those are libraries. This is a *plan*: daily streaks, hearts, leagues, and the dashboard showing you who's studying. It sits on top of what you have; most churches use both." |
| "Our people won't use an app" | "That's the 5-minute bet. The mechanics are the same ones keeping half a billion people on a language app — they work on churched and unchurched thumbs alike. And the dashboard tells you the truth in week one." |
| "$99/month is real money" | "It's $2 a seat for 50 people — less than one Saturday breakfast for the leadership team. And it's month-to-month: if January's dashboard is empty, cancel in February and you've lost one breakfast." |
| "Is it doctrinally safe?" | "The Statement of Faith is in the app, on the profile page, in plain sight — Nicene core, audited against every course. Plus the denominational studies units cover the traditions honestly. You can read every word before you send the email." |
| "Let me pray about it / check with the board" | "Of course — it's a discipleship decision, not a software decision. I'll hold your founding-rate spot through [date + 10 days]. One thing that helps boards: the 90-second demo video — forward it, I'll join the board call if useful." |

## 5 · Pilot terms sheet (one page, hand it over at the demo)

- **Term:** month-to-month; pilot discount locks for 12 months
- **Seats:** up to 50 active seats (unused seats don't expire; swap freely)
- **Price:** $99/month flat — unlimited hearts, all 18 courses, dashboard included
- **Onboarding:** congregation email template + seats live within 48h of signup
- **Success criteria (review together at day 60):** ≥40% of seats active in a week; ≥25% with a 7-day streak; measurable Bible-chapter coverage growth
- **Exit:** cancel any month, export the member list, no lock-in — *"if it's not working, you should fire us."*

## 6 · The Advent tie-in (this is why the timing is now)

Every pilot conversation happens inside the Advent/Christmas gift campaign window (Nov 23–Jan 6):
- **For the congregation:** "Start Advent with it — Genesis 1–3 as a congregation, the dashboard fills by Christmas."
- **For the pastor's gifting:** every pilot church gets **5 gift codes free** ($150 value) — "for your leadership team, on us" (order-bump inventory, costs you nothing but unlocks the year-long plan conversation).
- **The new-year close:** "January 1 is the single biggest Bible-reading start date of the year. If the seats are live in December, you catch that wave."
